How can we help you today?

How do I create a trip?

Go to the "Create Trip" tab in your navigation menu. Enter your trip name, dates, cover photo, and the cities you plan to visit. It's that easy!

How does the budget feature work?

Inside any of your trips, click on "Billing" or the "Budget" section. You can set a total budget, log expenses, and instantly generate a downloadable invoice with tax and discount settings.

Can I share my itinerary?

Yes! In the Community tab, you can create a post and optionally link it to one of your existing trips so others can get inspired by your journey.
